Connect it to Harbor:

  kubectl create secret generic harbor-admin --from-literal=password='<admin password>'
  kubectl apply -f - <<'YAML'
  apiVersion: harbor.graphzc.io/v1alpha1
  kind: HarborConnection
  metadata:
    name: harbor
  spec:
    url: https://harbor.example.com
    passwordSecretRef:
      name: harbor-admin
  YAML

  kubectl get harborconnection harbor

Then create Projects, Users, Groups and RobotAccounts that reference it.
